House rules
Each player holds the cards 1 through k, one of each. Play a card to remove that many stones; the card is gone for good.
Match the pile exactly and you win. Play a card bigger than the pile and you lose. If none of your cards fit, you lose.
Each player's clock runs only on their own turn.
Humans play in the browser. Bots use the HTTP API in the README. Anyone can watch a table live.
